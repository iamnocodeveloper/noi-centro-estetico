## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Atmosphere
This design system crafts an aura of medical precision blended with holistic sanctuary luxury. Designed for high-end aesthetic medicine and wellness clientele in Cuenca, Ecuador, the aesthetic balances scientific authority with serene sensory indulgence. The tone is discreet, reassuring, and undeniably prestigious.

### Emotional Response
The interface must evoke immediate calm, effortless confidence, and tactile indulgence. The visual cadence replaces clinical sterility with polished warmth, making high-end clinical dermatology, anti-aging therapies, and spa treatments feel like an essential self-care ritual.

### Design Movement: Botanical Luxury & Frosted Glassmorphism
The aesthetic synthesizes warm editorial minimalism with soft glassmorphic depth. Translucent overlays, warm champagne accents, organic sage grounding, and refined serif typography define the presentation.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke couture editorial spreads.
- **Micro-Depth:** Subtle ambient glows, frosted card layers, and delicate 1px golden hair-line borders create tactile distinction without visual noise.
- **Organic Flow:** Balanced, generous breathing room with softened geometry reflecting physical wellness environments.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette draws inspiration from mineral jade, serene Andean botanical flora, polished champagne gold, and warm alabaster. 

- **Primary (`#2D4B43` - Deep Sage / Botanical Jade):** The grounding anchor. Communicates medical authority, natural balance, and calm luxury. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis text, and active navigational indicators.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Polished Warm Champagne / Muted Gold):** The touch of luxury. Deployed across badges, secondary actions, pricing anchors, micro-glows, and key conversion focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#E8D3B9` - Pale Cashmere / Gold Veil):** Used for delicate backdrops, warm frosted card layers, active selection fills, and fine borders.
- **Neutral (`#1A1F1E` - Graphite Charcoal):** Ensures sharp, legible typography and high-contrast accessibility on bright, luminous backgrounds without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface System (Light Mode Primary)
- **Base Canvas:** `#FAF8F5` (Polished Warm Pearl).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Panels):** `#FFFFFF` with 85% opacity backdrop-filter or full opacity.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Overlays & Modals):** `#FFFFFF` with gentle golden rim lighting (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`).
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** `#E8F0EC` (Soft Sage Foam) for treatment highlight chips and soothing contextual panels.

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy
The typographic structure couples the classical, high-contrast romance of `Playfair Display` with the crystal-clear, ergonomic readability of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for prestige moments, titles of specialized aesthetic protocols, and hero headlines. Gives the client the feeling of browsing an exclusive medical publication.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry and rounded terminals that soften technical treatment explanations, consultation steps, pricing cards, and appointment schedules.
- **Casing & Micro-Labels:** Treatment categories and status markers (e.g., "FACIAL DERMATOLOGY", "RESERVA EXCLUSIVA") utilize uppercase `label-sm` with widened letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to sustain an ultra-luxury editorial presence.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Centered max-width of `1280px` with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer margins of `3rem`. Provides ample lateral negative space, allowing imagery and clinical copy to breathe comfortably.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container with `gutter: 1.25rem` and outer margins of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid container with `gutter-mobile: 1rem` and `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`. Ensures single-column flow for service discovery and booking workflows.

### Spacing Rhythm
The spatial philosophy relies on generous micro- and macro-padding to emulate the tranquility of an architectural spa:
- Use `space-xl` for sectional transitions, giving medical equipment introductions and physician profiles distinct separation.
- Component-internal layouts favor comfortable touch targets: cards adopt `space-lg` internal cushioning, while mobile interactive zones adhere strictly to a minimum 48px vertical footprint.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Glow & Frosted Layers
Depth in this design system rejects heavy, muddy dropshadows in favor of ethereal illumination and botanical refraction.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral pearl ground (`#FAF8F5`) without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Treatment Cards & Ambient Panels):** Translucent frosted layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), backed by a soft, diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(45, 75, 67, 0.08)`. Outlined with a 1px boundary of `rgba(232, 211, 185, 0.5)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Highlight Treatments):** Amplified elevation pairing a golden-champagne rim lighting with a sage halo: `0 14px 40px -10px rgba(45, 75, 67, 0.12), 0 0 16px 2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Booking Overlays & Diagnostic Modals):** Crisp glass substrate with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and deep grounding shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(26, 31, 30, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

### Rounded Geometry Scale
The shape language uses Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` base), striking a balance between clinical precision and anatomical, organic grace.

- **Micro Elements (Badges, Buttons, Inputs):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Clean, defined, comfortable to touch.
- **Standard Surfaces (Cards, Media Wrappers, Dropdowns):** `16px` (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`). Harmonizes structural grid alignment with soft corners.
- **Macro Containers (Sheet Overlays, Hero Banners, Modals):** `24px` (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`). Evokes smooth, sculpted interior architecture.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Filter pills and appointment status chips may use full roundedness (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical tags from interactive actionable cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion Actions
- **Primary Action (Agendar Cita / Procedimiento):** Solid `#2D4B43` background with luminous `#FAF8F5` text. Hover state gently shifts to a subtle gold rim (`box-shadow: 0 0 14px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`). Minimum height of `48px` on mobile, padded with `space-lg` horizontal bounds.
- **Secondary Luxury Action (Ver Tratamiento):** Transparent surface with a 1.5px border in `#C5A059`, text in `#2D4B43`. On hover, fills with `rgba(232, 211, 185, 0.2)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Plain `#2D4B43` text with subtle gold underline accent that expands on hover.

### Service & Specialist Cards
- Built with a glassmorphic white veneer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), framed by a subtle 1px border (`#E8D3B9`).
- Features a designated photo slot with top-radius curvature matching the card (`16px`), paired with generous `space-lg` padding for protocol details, price benchmarks in champagne gold (`#C5A059`), and estimated session durations.

### Input Fields & Consultation Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(45, 75, 67, 0.2)`.
- Focus State: Smooth transition to a dual ring (`border-color: #2D4B43`, `outline: 2px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`).
- Typography inside inputs adheres strictly to `body-md` in `#1A1F1E`, with placeholders set in a softened graphite tint.

### Badges & Category Chips
- Background: Soft sage foam (`#E8F0EC`) or warm champagne mist (`rgba(232, 211, 185, 0.3)`).
- Text: `#2D4B43` in uppercase `label-sm` with letter spacing `0.08em`.
- Shape: Rounded-pill with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Date Selectors
- Custom controls using a 2px stroke in `#2D4B43`.
- Active states deploy a rich `#2D4B43` fill with an inner golden checkmark or center pip (`#C5A059`), avoiding default browser controls entirely.
- Calendar date selectors highlight availability with soft sage circles and selected appointments with `#2D4B43` accompanied by a golden halo.
